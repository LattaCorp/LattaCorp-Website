What areas do you serve?

We serve Central Kissimmee, Campbell, and Upper Poinciana, with most of our work being in The Oaks Community.

How often will you mow my lawn?

Our maintenance plans are weekly or biweekly (every other week). We highly recommend weekly maintenance for a much healthier lawn. Weekly-cut lawns tend to need fewer chemicals and less fertilizer, and they naturally crowd out weeds. During the colder months, weekly properties are cut biweekly so we don't shock the grass.

What happens if it rains on my service day?

We reschedule for the next available day so your lawn is cut properly. We never rush our work, because we want quality results. If a property is wet, we delay service until it is dry so we don't cause compaction or ruts that would leave lasting damage.

Do I need to be home during service?

No. As long as our crew can reach the yard, you don't need to be there. Our employees are trained to work carefully around sensitive areas such as sprinklers, utilities, and plants without causing damage.

How do I get a quote?

Use the Request a Quote form on the Services page. You can add photos of your yard so we can estimate remotely. If you want a quick estimate, enter the square footage of your entire property into the calculator on the Services page. It may not be exact, but it will give you a rough idea of what maintenance will look like.

I live in an HOA community. How do I report an issue?

Use the Service Requests tab, choose your community, and tell us what you need or what went wrong.

Welcome to Latta Corp!

Transforming Lawns. Growing Communities.

Latta Corp of Central Florida began with one man and a mower. Carlos Latalladi started cutting grass and maintaining lawns simply to help out his friendly neighbors. What began as small acts of service grew into a full commitment to beautifying entire communities, one yard at a time.

Today, that same neighborly spirit drives everything we do. From residential lawns to commercial properties and HOA communities, we turn outdoor spaces into vibrant, well-maintained landscapes with dependable, high-quality care and a personal touch.

What we take care of

Lawns

Mowing, edging, string trimming, and a clean blow-off when we finish.

Beds and mulch

Fresh mulch, tidy beds, and weed control that keeps them looking sharp.

Trees and shrubs

Hedge trimming and tree pruning, done the way each plant needs it.

Come work with us

We're a Kissimmee landscaping crew caring for commercial properties and homes. We like our work done right, done on time, and done with pride.

Some experience is great, but we're happy to train the right person. What matters most is that you show up, work hard, and care how it looks when you leave.

What you'll be doing

Depending on your experience, a day might include:

  • Mowing and lawn upkeep
  • String trimming and edging along walks, drives, and beds
  • Hedge and shrub trimming
  • Mulching and keeping beds neat
  • Tree pruning and basic tree care
  • Blowing and cleanup
  • Learning to spot different plants and turf, and how to care for each
  • Keeping equipment in shape and work areas tidy

Hours

Part-time to start, at least 16 hours a week. In our busy seasons it can reach 30 to 40. Full-time spots aren't open right now, but when they are, our part-time crew gets first look based on reliability, quality of work, and commitment.

Who does well here

  • Shows up on time, every time
  • Works fast without cutting corners
  • Notices the details
  • Handles Florida heat and hard physical work
  • Wants to learn and keep getting better
  • Works well alone and with a crew
  • Respects customers, their property, and our equipment

No experience? That's okay. We care more about your attitude and work ethic than what's on a résumé.

Nice to have

High school diploma or equivalent, English, about 2 years of landscaping experience, and a pesticide applicator's license. You'll need to be able to commute to Kissimmee, FL 34746.

A note on safety

We work on people's homes and community properties, so trust matters. We can't consider applicants with violent convictions, such as assault or other dangerous offenses.

We want people who'll grow with a growing company. If that's you, download the application and send it our way.
