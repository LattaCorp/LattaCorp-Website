Terms of Service

The simple rules for working together. Please read them before accepting a quote.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to all lawn care and landscaping work done by Latta Corp of Central Florida for residential, HOA and commercial clients.

By signing a quote or estimate, signing up for service, or letting us start work, you agree to these terms. If you have a signed contract with us that says something different, the contract wins.

2. Quotes and estimates

A Quote is a fixed price for the work it describes. Once you sign it, that is the price you pay.

An Estimate is our best price for work where the final cost depends on what we find, like roots, drainage or hidden damage. If the price needs to go up, we contact you before doing the extra work.

Quotes and estimates are good until the Valid Until date shown on them. Anything not listed is extra work and is priced separately.

3. Deposits

Some jobs need a deposit, shown on the quote. We schedule the work once the deposit is paid, and it is taken off your final invoice. If you cancel after we have bought materials for your job, we may keep the part of the deposit that covers them.

4. Scheduling and weather

Recurring service happens on your scheduled day, Monday through Saturday. Rain, storms or wet ground can delay a visit; we move it to the next available day. During the cooler months, weekly lawns may be cut every other week so we don't stress the grass. A weather delay is not a missed visit.

5. Getting to the work

Please make sure our crew can reach the area we are working on: gates unlocked or a gate code given to us, pets inside or secured, and toys, hoses and loose items picked up. If we can't get in on your service day, the visit may still be charged.

6. Paying your invoice

Invoices are due by the Payment Due date shown on them. We accept Zelle, cash and check, and we let you know when other ways to pay are available. Payments are applied to your oldest balance first.

7. Late payments

Residential accounts: a $10 late fee is added when an invoice is 10 days past due. If it is still unpaid 30 days past due, a finance charge of 1.5% per month is also added to the unpaid balance.

HOA and commercial accounts: past-due balances are charged 1.5% per month.

We may pause service on accounts that are more than 30 days past due until the balance is paid.

8. Cancelling service

You can cancel recurring service at any time with 7 days' notice before your next visit, by call, text or email. You only pay for work already done. One-time jobs can be cancelled before we start; see Deposits above.

9. If something isn't right

Let us know within 48 hours of a visit and we will come back and fix it at no charge.

10. Plants, sod and materials

New plants and sod we install are guaranteed for 30 days. If one dies in that time, we replace it once, as long as it was watered the way we told you and wasn't harmed by freezes, storms, flooding, pests, disease, pets or vehicles. Mulch, rock and other materials are not guaranteed against fading, settling or washing out.

11. Damage

We work carefully around your property. If our crew damages something, tell us within 48 hours and we will repair or replace it.

We are not responsible for hidden or unmarked items, such as sprinkler lines and heads, buried cables, invisible pet fences or landscape lighting, unless they were marked or pointed out to us before the work. Items that were already broken or worn out are not covered.

12. Weed and pest treatments

Treatments are applied following the product label. Keep children and pets off treated areas until they are dry. Results depend on weather and conditions and can take a few weeks to show.

13. HOA and commercial properties

For HOA and commercial properties, the signed contract or board-approved proposal sets the scope and schedule. These terms cover anything it doesn't.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page applies to new quotes and to service from the date it is posted.
